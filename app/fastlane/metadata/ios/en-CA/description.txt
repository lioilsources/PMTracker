PMTracker is a time tracker for construction and field-service teams.

• Start and stop time tracking on the job you are assigned to
• Your position is checked against the job's geofence only when you start and stop; coordinates are discarded and only the result is stored
• Outside the geofence you can continue with a reason, and your manager is informed
• Managers create jobs and see reports of hours per team member; admins manage users and roles
